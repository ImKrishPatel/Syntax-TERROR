      Sound guide · learn what you’re hearing

      More equal-pitch pulses mean more activity. One lower tone means zero activity. Rising tones mean above usual; falling tones mean below usual; two steady tones mean typical. Two low tones mark missing data, not zero. A separate low pair marks an unavailable comparison.

      Examples always play their named pattern. Activity scales within the listening year, so compare values—not loudness or pulse counts—between regions or years.

      Sound represents data, not recordings of actual fires. Turn spoken descriptions off if you prefer your screen reader. Summary speech follows playback speed, with a fixed 0.5-second pause after periods and semicolons.

      Can the two sensors be trusted as one record?

      What is a hotspot? A satellite pixel that was hot enough to be an active fire when the satellite passed over. MODIS has seen them since 2000 at 1 km, VIIRS since 2012 at 375 m. This record puts both on the VIIRS scale so that 2005 can be compared with 2025.

      What is the score? How far a month is from the average of that same calendar month, counted in standard deviations (σ). Around 0 is normal. 2 or more is unusual.
