        Keyword guide · what to say

        Replace “Year” and “Month” with your choice, such as “April 2024 Fire Energy”. Use a year within the selected date range.

          Click Speak for each command. Commands use the selected region, enable spoken descriptions, and unmute audio. Fire Energy means summed fire radiative power, measured in megawatts. Overview and Strongest Ano use the current measure.

          Your browser may send speech to its recognition service. Microphone access starts only when you click Speak or use its keyboard shortcut. Say “Leave” to cancel.

          Sound guide · learn what you’re hearing

          More equal-pitch pulses mean more activity. One lower tone means zero activity. Rising tones mean above usual; falling tones mean below usual; two steady tones mean typical. Two low tones mark missing data, not zero. A separate low pair marks an unavailable comparison.

          Examples always play their named pattern. Activity scales within the listening year, so compare values—not loudness or pulse counts—between regions or years.

          Sound represents data, not recordings of actual fires. Turn spoken descriptions off if you prefer your screen reader. Summary speech follows playback speed, with a fixed 0.5-second pause after periods and semicolons.

          Can the two sensors be trusted as one record?

          What is a hotspot? A satellite pixel that was hot enough to be an active fire when the satellite passed over. Wildfires are the most common cause, but volcanoes, gas flares and farm burning show up too. MODIS has seen them since 2000 at 1 km, VIIRS since 2012 at 375 m. This record uses MODIS for every year, so 2006 and 2026 are counted the same way; VIIRS is used to check it (above).

          What is the score? How far a month is from the average of that same calendar month, counted in standard deviations (σ). Around 0 is normal. 2 or more is unusual.
